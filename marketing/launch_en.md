# Decision Arena: find out what would change your mind

I built a small tool for the two-option decisions that keep circling in your head — stay or go, buy or wait. It does not tell you what will happen. It shows you which of your own assumptions has to move before your answer flips.

Take the bundled fictional example: a job offer from a tiny startup versus staying where you are. With the default weights, A scores 70.7 and B scores 63.6 out of 100 — A leads by 7.1.

Now drag the weight on "income certainty" from 4 to 7. The scores become A 65.3 and B 66.5. The answer flips.

Nothing about the world changed. Three points of weight did it, and that is the useful part. The tool shows the two sides even out once income certainty falls to about 39.4% of total weight — below that, A wins again. So the original answer was two notches from flipping. It then names income certainty as the assumption worth checking first, plus a small action you can finish within a week.

Every number comes from your own 0–10 ratings and your own weights. It is not a success rate and it is not a prediction. It has no opinion about which option you should pick. Nor is it suitable for medical, legal, or financial decisions — those need a qualified professional.

Zero dependencies, no login, no upload, open source and free. Try it now: https://tbj0623.github.io/decision-arena-lab/
